# Sterling & Cole Demo

Build a premium, high-trust website demo for a fictional law firm called "Sterling & Cole Law Group."

DESIGN DIRECTION:
- Sophisticated, authoritative, and trustworthy — dark navy or charcoal background with a warm gold or brass accent, crisp white/off-white text, classic serif headlines paired with a clean modern sans-serif for body text
- Should feel like a top-tier legal firm: confident, established, premium — not corporate-boring, not template-generic
- Generous whitespace, structured grid layout, subtle elegant motion (fade/slide reveals on scroll)
- Fully responsive, mobile-first

PAGES / SECTIONS (single-page or lightly multi-page is fine):
1. Hero — strong headline about protecting clients' interests / results-driven representation; subheading on experience and track record; primary CTA "Schedule a Consultation", secondary CTA "Our Practice Areas"
2. Practice Areas — Personal Injury, Family Law, Business/Corporate Law, Real Estate Law, Criminal Defense, Estate Planning — as elegant interactive cards
3. Why Choose Us — trust signals: years of combined experience, case results/track record, client-first approach, free consultations, availability
4. Meet the Attorneys — short bios with photos (placeholder), credentials, bar admissions
5. Client Testimonials — 3-4 short client reviews with star ratings
6. Case Results / Track Record — a few stat highlights (e.g. "$2M+ recovered", "500+ cases won") presented as bold animated counters
7. Consultation/Contact section — form (name, phone, email, case type, brief description), office address, phone, hours, embedded map placeholder
8. Footer with disclaimers, quick links, social links

Make it feel like a real, established law firm website — polished, authoritative, and built to convert visitors into consultation bookings. Use tasteful subtle animations, refined hover states on cards and buttons.
